### Fire

Should we do active steps to minimize the risk of the hardware igniting?
- quality PSU with a native connector, don't smother the airflow, don't run it in a sealed cabinet, and put a smoke detector in the room.
    
- Specifically regarding the RTX 4090:
	- The 4090 (and other 40-series cards) uses a 12VHPWR connector rated for 600W continuous. Shortly after launch, there were multiple documented cases of the power cable or adapter burning or melting, traced to a specific cause: the adapter's solder points and internal bridges were too thin and would break when the cable was bent or kinked near the connector, and that weak connection created a fire risk.
